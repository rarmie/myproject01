Jobs page, chosen 9 Oct 2026: Midnight + Outfit, dark and light

Final visual design for /jobs. The layout (list + wide detail, selection in ?job) is unchanged from 2026-10-02-jobs-layout-wide-detail.html. This changes the look and the page header. Dark is the default, with a toggle to light.

  • Font: Outfit everywhere, headings at 700.
  • Accent: emerald #34d399 (dark text) in dark mode; the app's existing indigo oklch(0.511 0.262 277) (white text) in light mode.
  • Status colours: Applied follows the accent; Interview amber; Offer fuchsia in both themes; Rejected red; Wishlist grey. Lightness adjusts per theme.
  • Top bar: the lighter, "raised" layer in both themes, with a clear line under it. Holds only nav, Add, theme and help.
  • Content: no bordered box, no page padding. List and detail share one shade (dark: deepest navy; light: soft grey).
  • Title row: "Job Applications" left, search right, one row, no subtitle or count, no line under it.
  • Divider: one line between list and detail, fading in from transparent below the title row.
