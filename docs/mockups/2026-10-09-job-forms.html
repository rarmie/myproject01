Add / Edit job forms, chosen 9 Oct 2026

Applies the jobs page design (2026-10-09-jobs-page-midnight.html) to the two job dialogs. Shown: the Add dialog in dark mode, mid-fill (Role left empty, Link focused). The Edit dialog uses the same fields, labels, errors and footer, without the paste panel, plus a required Applied date.

  • Header: title plus a one-line description. No separator line (tried and reverted).
  • Autofill: a dashed panel at the top with an icon, a one-line explanation, the paste box, the length hint and the Extract button. Chosen over a collapsed one-line version, so the AI feature stays visible.
  • Fields: required fields marked with a red *, others tagged "optional"; placeholders are examples, not repeated labels; Requirements has a "One per line." hint.
  • Status: readable names with their status colour dot, as in the job list.
  • Errors and focus: theme colours (destructive outline and message, accent focus ring), and each label is linked to its input.
  • Footer: tinted band with a line above it, Cancel and Save on the right.
